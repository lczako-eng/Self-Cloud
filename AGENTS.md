## Ground rules (non-negotiable)
1. **Attribution.** Sole creator/inventor = **Laszlo Czako**. Never attribute this work to an
   AI. Commit under the owner's git identity; AI contributors go only in `Co-Authored-By:` trailers.
2. **Spelling is canon.** The product family is **JEFFEREY** (double-e) — it matches the filed
   trademark. Do **not** "correct" it to "Jeffrey." No "Jeff." No "butler."
3. **IP records are append-only history.** Never rewrite, backdate, or delete prior concept
   records, dates, or authorship files — they establish priority. Add; don't overwrite.
4. **No private data in this public repo — ever.** No addresses, phone, financial/card, gov
   filings, or contents of personal files. Government PDFs stay in the owner's private store;
   the repo carries only an index of application numbers/dates.
5. **Propose on branches; the human merges.** AIs don't push to `main` without the owner's say.
6. **Separate concerns:** `docs/` = canon, specs, records · code lives in its own dir.

## Collaboration protocol (so the two engines converge, not diverge)
- Leave a dated entry in `docs/BUILD_JOURNAL.md` after any substantive work: what you did, what
  you decided, what's open. Read the other engine's last entries before starting.
- Disagreements get flagged with `⚑` in-line and raised to the owner — not silently "fixed."
- One truth: if something conflicts with `CANON.md`, CANON wins or CANON gets updated by the
  owner. No parallel forks of the concept.
