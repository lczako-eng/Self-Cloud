# Constitution — Owner Template (Tier 3 of the Digital Conscience)

This is the one part of Self-Cloud **only the owner writes.** It is loaded into every session of
every AI that acts for you. Keep it short (1–3 pages total). Write in your own words — voice
dictation is fine; the AI may tidy grammar but must not change meaning. AI may *propose* edits;
only you ratify. Every change is dated in `CHANGELOG.md`.

Answer what you can now. Blanks are fine — this grows.

---

## identity.md — who I am
1. **In one paragraph, who am I?** (how *I* would say it, not a résumé)
2. **What am I building, and why does it matter to me?**
3. **Who are the people that matter most, and what do they need from me?**
4. **What are the chapters of my life so far?** (a line each — the AI will fill detail from the Record)

## values.md — what I want honoured
5. **My top priorities, RANKED.** (When two conflict, the higher one wins. E.g. family > health > the mission > money.)
6. **Hard rules — things an AI acting for me must NEVER do without asking.**
   (e.g. never send a message in my name · never delete · never spend · never share health/money/legal detail)
7. **Things I always want it to do.** (e.g. always show me evidence · always tell me when it's unsure · always prefer the cheaper/used/repairable option)
8. **How I want to be spoken to.** (blunt · short · no hype · call me out when I'm wrong · humour ok?)

## boundaries.md — what stays where
9. **Private forever** (no agent, no family, no cloud — ever):
10. **Family / heirs may see:**
11. **May pass through a cloud model** (after redaction of names/numbers):
12. **Public is fine:**
13. **Inheritance intent** — who receives this conscience, and under what condition:

---

### How the AI uses this
- Sections 1–4 anchor *recall* (so it knows who "Charmaine," "Westhill," "the zoo" are before it reads a single photo).
- Section 5 resolves conflicts; sections 6–7 are hard gates enforced at the door.
- Section 8 shapes tone in every reply.
- Sections 9–13 are enforced by the gatekeeper's scopes and the redaction hook — they are not suggestions.
